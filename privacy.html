Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026.

Oura Local Downloader is a private tool operated by its sole user to download and analyze their own Oura data. It is not offered as a public service.

After the operator authorizes access through Oura OAuth, the tool retrieves sleep, HRV, heart rate, readiness, activity, stress, SpO2, resilience, sleep-time recommendations, tags, workouts, and sessions where available and authorized. Individual heart-rate readings are optional. It does not request email or profile access.

The tool contacts Oura directly. Credentials and access/refresh tokens stay in process memory and are not intentionally saved or logged by the tool. The browser uses a temporary callback listening only on the local computer.

Downloaded data is stored as JSON and CSV in a folder selected by the operator. The tool has no analytics, advertising, telemetry, or third-party data transmission. Operating-system backups, browser behavior, cloud-synced folders, and services the operator separately chooses are governed by their own settings and policies.

The operator controls retention and may delete exported files at any time and revoke the application through Oura account settings. Deleting local exports does not delete the original data held by Oura.

This documentation site receives no health data from the downloader. Its host may process ordinary website access logs under its own privacy policy.

Because the operator is the only user, questions and requests are handled directly by that operator. No other users' data is collected.
